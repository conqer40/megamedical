Primary Production Hub

Advanced Cleanroom Production & EO Sterilization

MegaMedical operates under ISO 13485 medical device quality systems with certified cleanrooms compliant with ISO Class 7 and Class 8 standards. From automated three-part syringe assembly to precision PVC tube extrusion for hemodialysis bloodlines, our production integrates high-volume efficiency with zero-defect consistency.

Every batch of invasive consumables undergoes 100% ethylene oxide (EO) gas sterilization with comprehensive biological indicator verification before release for export packing.
