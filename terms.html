Commercial Terms & Logistics Protocol

Transparent, standard commercial conditions based on international Incoterms® 2020 rules, secure financial mechanisms, and robust ocean shipping procedures.

⚓

Incoterms® 2020 & Seaports

  • ■ FOB Alexandria Port: Strategic Mediterranean terminal connecting to European and North African destinations.
  • ■ FOB Sokhna Port: Modern Red Sea terminal offering rapid transit times to Arabian Gulf, East Africa, and Asian corridors.
  • ■ CIF / CFR: Competitive maritime freight rates provided upon proforma confirmation.
💳

International Payment Terms

  • ■ Telegraphic Transfer (T/T): Milestone payments (e.g. 30% advance deposit with balance against shipping documents).
  • ■ Letter of Credit (LC): 100% Irrevocable, confirmed LC at sight issued by first-tier international banking institutions.
  • ■ Escrow and tender financing supported for ministry of health supply agreements.
📦

Export Packaging & Mix-Load

  • ■ Heavy-duty 5-ply & 7-ply corrugated moisture-resistant export cartons designed for rough ocean transit.
  • ■ Specialized One-Stop FCL Mix-Load combines gloves, syringes, masks, and catheters in a single 20ft or 40ft container.
  • ■ Palletized or floor-loaded options to maximize container CBM volume.
📋

Legal & Export Documentation

  • ■ Legalized Commercial Invoice and Detailed Packing List (Net/Gross weights & CBM).
  • ■ Official Egyptian Chamber of Commerce Certificate of Origin (COO).
  • ■ Preferential trade certificates: EUR.1 (Europe) & COMESA (Africa customs duty exemptions).
  • ■ Complete certified copies of CE conformity and ISO 13485 registration.
⏱️

Lead Times & Order Cycles

  • ■ Typical manufacturing lead time is 15 to 30 calendar days following advance payment confirmation.
  • ■ Immediate ex-factory stock release available for core standard examination lines.
  • ■ Dedicated shipment tracking updates from cleanroom packing to vessel boarding.
🏷️

Private Labeling (OEM)

  • ■ Custom private label packaging and box artwork design available for volume hospital contracts.
  • ■ Full regulatory compliance support including barcode generation and language-specific labeling.
  • ■ Final technical dimensions and certifications confirmed formally on the Proforma Invoice.
